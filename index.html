    About EBCA

    From Sankalp to Sanskriti

    A socio-cultural philanthropic organisation incepted in 2016, EBCA has grown into East Bengaluru's flagship cultural celebration — serving the Whitefield–KR Puram corridor and known as the "Maddox Square of Bangalore".

    2016

    The beginning

    EBCA is incepted as a socio-cultural philanthropic organisation in East Bengaluru.

    2018

    A sacred yatra

    Inaugurated by Locket Chatterjee with a blessing message from the Hon'ble Prime Minister. Bengaluru's first Anandamela and a 20-ft Kali murti.

    2020

    Dharma in difficult times

    Kept Bhakti uninterrupted through digital celebrations and virtual festivals during the pandemic.

    2022

    Seva beyond celebration

    10,000+ meals served, Channapatna toys promoted (Vocal for Local), and multiple awards received.

    2024

    A grand cultural confluence

    Widely recognised as the "Maddox Square of Bangalore" — Dhaki & Dhunuchi Naach competitions and a landmark performance by Bhoomi.

    2026

    9 glorious years

    College Street book fair with 5,000+ Bengali books, Patachitra art, and a theme pandal by Bangalore Chitra Kala Parishad.

    ⚠️ Prototype access. This sign-in only shows or hides this section — it is not real security. Treat anything entered here as visible to anyone who knows this page exists. Move real financial data behind a server before you rely on it.
    🔒 Everything happens on this device. Files are read in your browser and the ledger is stored only in this browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Use Export to keep a permanent copy.

    Step 1 — Add the receipts

    Drag in as many as you like. Images are read with on-device OCR; PDFs have their text read directly. You can also just add rows by hand at the bottom of this list.

    How this saves time

    1. Save the receipts out of WhatsApp into a folder (you're already looking at them).
    2. Drop the whole folder here at once — no need to do them one by one.
    3. Scan the table for anything odd, fix it, then Add all to ledger.
    4. At the end of the day, Export Excel and file it.

    For the money that lands in the bank, skip all of that — import the statement and let the bank's own record do the work.
