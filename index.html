Our journey

From Sankalp to Sanskriti

Since 2018, EBCA has grown into East Bengaluru's flagship cultural celebration — a Sanatani socio-cultural space where traditions are preserved, festivals are experienced and culture is lived.

2018

The beginning of a sacred yatra

Inaugurated by Locket Chatterjee and blessed with a message from the Hon'ble Prime Minister. Introduced Bengaluru's first Anandamela.

2020

Dharma in difficult times

Kept Bhakti uninterrupted through digital celebrations and virtual festivals during the pandemic.

2022

Seva beyond celebration

10,000+ meals served, Channapatna toys promoted (Vocal for Local), and multiple awards received.

2024

A grand cultural confluence

Widely recognised as the "Maddox Square of Bangalore" — Dhaki & Dhunuchi Naach competitions and a landmark performance by Bhoomi.

2025

Towards greater glory

College Street comes to Bengaluru with 5,000+ Bengali books, Patachitra art, and a theme pandal by Chitra Kala Parishad.
